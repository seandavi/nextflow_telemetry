# Metadata source catalog vocabulary

**Source**: A maintained upstream resource from which we obtain metadata or an
inventory of metadata. It can be known before any of its contents are acquired.

**Asset**: An identifiable table, tab, file or bundle within a source. Its role
can be an inventory, sample metadata, study metadata, profiles or another kind.

**Snapshot**: One immutable observation of an asset's original bytes at a named
version. A snapshot records what was acquired, not whether the assertions are
scientifically correct or appropriate to publish.

**Descriptor**: Our reviewed or unreviewed description of a source or asset,
including its location, access and licensing information. Descriptor corrections
do not change the history of acquired snapshots.

**Inventory**: Metadata about available studies, datasets or metadata resources;
not necessarily descriptive values about individual specimens.
